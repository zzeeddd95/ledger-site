Record a trade

Tap + and fill in the ticket. Buy, Sell and Close record entries in your journal; they never place orders with a broker. A fee you leave blank stays $0.00.

Try it with sample trades

Open Settings with the gear on Today, tap The Ledger card at the top (Journal & data), and turn on Sample data under Try it out. Sample data is labelled on every screen and its prices are simulated. Changes to it stay in memory; turn Sample data off to return to your own journal.

On iPhone Duo

The cover screen shows your day at a glance. Unfold, and a trade you pick opens beside your journal, Performance opens its pages beside its summary, and Settings shows its list beside the page you open. Folding or turning the Duo keeps your place.

Bring trades in

  • Trade emails: Settings › Trade emails connects the mailbox your Wealthsimple trade emails arrive in, with an app password from your mail provider. With Log automatically on, verified fills log themselves; anything unclear waits under Needs a look.
  • Import trades: Settings › Import trades opens a supported broker statement or a screenshot of a fill. Review the preview before tapping Import. Imports go to your own journal, so leave Sample data first.

Every setting explained

Each setting has a small circled i after its name. Tap it for a short explanation.

AI reads

AI reads are short notes on a trade or on the market, written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, and free for now, up to 20 a day on each phone. They start once you allow them: tap Turn on AI reads on Home, or open Settings › AI reads. The app shows what each read sends before you choose, and you can stop them there at any time.

Prices or AI reads are unavailable

You can still record trades and read plain summaries. Check your internet connection and try again, and check Settings › Privacy › Access; if it offers Get access, tap it. If the problem continues, email support with the message the app shows.
