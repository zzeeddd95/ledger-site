Privacy policy

Dated 24 November 2026

RookyTech Inc. provides The Ledger, a trading journal for recording and reviewing trades. It does not place broker orders. The Ledger has no ads, no advertising analytics and no tracking.

Your journal stays on your device and in your own iCloud

Your journal of record (your trades, notes, imports and settings) is stored on your device. The Ledger's server does not host or sync your journal. You can export a CSV or share information yourself. Optional AI reads send selected trade information, as described below.

The app also keeps one backup copy of your journal in your own iCloud Drive, in The Ledger's private app folder, so you can restore it on a new iPhone. It is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; The Ledger's server never sees it. Mailbox passwords, a Claude key you added and the state of email imports are not backed up; they stay on the phone. iCloud backup is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings › Journal & data.

Trade emails

If you connect a mailbox, the app reads supported Wealthsimple trade emails directly from your mail provider, read-only, and processes them on your device. Only messages from wealthsimple.com are opened. Your mailbox sign-in, connection details and email-import history stay on your device; they are not sent to The Ledger's server. Emails waiting under Needs a look are kept on the device until resolved, for up to 30 days. Disconnecting the mailbox removes its saved access, import history and held emails from the app; trades already logged remain in your journal. The app never sends, moves or deletes mail in your mailbox.

Prices and charts

For prices and charts, the app sends The Ledger's server the symbols and contracts it needs. The server fetches prices from its market-data provider and may cache market responses. It does not keep a watchlist for your device.

Market Pulse and ticker news

Market Pulse uses public benchmark prices, headlines and economic release schedules, including public feeds from the Federal Reserve, the Bureau of Labor Statistics and the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Your tickers are chosen on your device from your open positions, recent trades and pinned tickers, up to five stock or ETF symbols, and the app sends those symbols to The Ledger's server for prices and news. The server gets ticker news from its news provider and caches public news and commentary in memory by ticker.

Market Pulse commentary is an AI read (see below), so it is written only after you allow AI reads. Those requests contain your tickers and public market information only, such as index quotes, headline excerpts and official release schedules. Catalyst Replay combines public events with your recorded fills on your device.

When a news item includes publisher artwork, the app loads the image from its host, which receives the usual connection information for that request.

AI reads

AI reads are short, labelled notes on a trade or on the market, written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. They are off until you allow them: the first time, the app shows what each read sends and asks Allow AI reads or Not now. Nothing is sent before you allow them, and you can stop them at any time in Settings › AI reads.

A trade read sends that trade's figures, setup, tags and notes over HTTPS to The Ledger's server, which passes them to Anthropic to write the read. With Settings › AI reads on Automatic, opening a closed trade can request a read, and an open trade is read when you tap Read this trade so far. A market read on Home or Your Market Pulse sends public index quotes, official releases and up to five of your tickers. Your install ID is not sent to Anthropic. Choose On this iPhone or Off to stop server AI reads; plain summaries remain available either way.

If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings › AI reads › Your Claude key, AI reads on that phone use your key, and Anthropic bills you for them.

The Ledger's server processes the trade information and the response in memory to return the read; it does not keep your prompt or the read's text, except that reads for the app owner's own devices may be cached to avoid repeating the same read. Anthropic's handling is separate: under its standard API policy, inputs and outputs are normally deleted within 30 days, and a different agreement, safety enforcement or legal requirements can change that period. See Anthropic's commercial data-retention policy.

What the server stores about your device

For prices, charts and AI reads, the app asks The Ledger's server for access for your device. Apple confirms to the server that this is the genuine app on a real device; no account, name or email is involved. To keep that access working, the server stores a random install ID, the public key and receipt from Apple's check, when it enrolled and when it was last used, the app version and how many AI reads it made each day. These records contain no journal entries, name, email, phone number or brokerage account.

Active installation records are kept while access is working. Records for expired access are removed after 90 days and revoked-access records after 400 days. After you remove this device's access, its record is removed by the next daily cleanup. Daily AI read counts are kept for 35 days and enrollment-event records for 48 hours.

Operational logs record technical information such as request status, outcome, an installation identifier, token counts and response time, to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service. They do not contain your AI prompt, the read's text or any key.

Removing your data

  • Mailbox access and held emails: Settings › Trade emails › Disconnect.
  • A Claude key you added yourself: Settings › AI reads › Your Claude key › Remove key, where shown.
  • Your journal: delete the app. Keychain items can outlast an app deletion, so use the controls above first.
  • Your iCloud backup: turn off iCloud backup in Settings › Journal & data, then in the iPhone's Settings open your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, choose The Ledger and delete its data.
  • Your device's records on The Ledger's server: Settings › Privacy › Remove this device's access. It also deletes a Claude key saved on the phone and turns AI reads off until you allow them again. Prices, charts and AI reads stop until you tap Get access, and your journal stays on your iPhone.
