The Ledger: Trading Journal

Built for iPhone Duo.

The Ledger is a stock and options trading journal made for both of the iPhone Duo's screens. Check your day on the cover screen, then unfold to see your trades, charts and performance side by side. It works on every other iPhone too.

Buy, Sell and Close record entries in your journal; The Ledger never places orders with a broker, and it does not give buy or sell recommendations. Your journal is stored on your device, with a backup in your own iCloud.
