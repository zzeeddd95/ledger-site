The Ledger: Trading Journal

Live P&L, prices and charts.

A live trading journal for stocks and options. Your P&L moves with the market, your positions follow real-time prices, and every trade gets a chart, a grade and an honest review.

Live P&L, all day

Today's net moves with the market, split into closed gains, open change and fees. Every open position shows its live gain or loss, in dollars and in R.

Real-time prices

Stock quotes from a real-time feed, refreshed about every 30 seconds while the market is open, each with its time. Options are priced live from the contract. A late quote is always labelled.

Live charts

1-minute bars with volume and VWAP, pre-market and after-hours included. Your entry, stop and target are drawn on, and every past trade sits one tap away.

Every trade graded

Entry, exit, risk and discipline, with the rule behind every point. Hindsight is kept apart, so it never changes a grade.

AI reads, free

Short, labelled reads of a trade or the market, written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. 20 trade reads a day on each phone, off until you allow them.

Made for iPhone Duo

Today's net and your positions on the cover screen. Unfold, and your trades, charts and performance open side by side. It works on every other iPhone too.

Private by design. Your journal lives on your iPhone, with a backup in your own iCloud that The Ledger's server never sees. No account or sign-in is needed.

The Ledger is a journal and review tool. Buy, Sell and Close record entries in your journal; it never places orders with a broker, and it does not give buy or sell advice.
